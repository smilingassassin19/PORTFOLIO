# Asset Management Guide for Puneeth M. Portfolio

You can place your real images directly in these folders:

- `public/images/profile/`: Place your portrait photo here as `puneeth.jpg`
- `public/images/projects/`: Place project screenshots here (e.g. `gkvk-agritech.jpg`, `spendsage.jpg`, `jarvis-ai.jpg`, etc.)
- `public/images/social-media/`: Place Instagram post/story/carousel graphics here
- `public/images/graphic-design/`: Place posters, banners, and brand identity assets here
- `public/images/advertisements/`: Place paid social ads, conversion banners, and product graphics here
- `public/images/video/`: Place reel thumbnails, video covers, and motion graphics frames here
- `public/images/uiux/`: Place Figma mobile and web interface mockups here

The portfolio is designed with an intelligent fallback system: until you drop your custom images, the site renders sharp, high-contrast dark/lime vector cards with typography, category badges, and geometric accents, ensuring zero broken image icons.
